1. **P1 must fix — Discharging a superseded facts gap can strand an already-entered claims layer and all its successors.** Location: `plan/designs/processing_layers_design.md:219–229` (§4.3), `:322–345` (§5.2), especially steps 2, 3 and 6.

   The new wait can leave a version with completed extraction but neither `embed_claim` nor a reconcile row. Advance cannot reliably release that wait: when facts are enabled, it discharges non-current unentered facts gaps, selects only the current version, and stops if that version has no facts gap. It never drains the pending claims tail of the superseded version. A facts gap may legitimately be discharged while the same version's claims gap is **entered**, and therefore must finish.

   A concrete schedule in a configured, facts-enabled living lineage:

   - v1 entered facts. Its observation/application barrier and `embed_claim` succeeded, but pause its full reconcile. This is a real scheduling window: `complete_entity_obs_flush` enqueues supersession and claim embedding separately (`src/rememberstack/spine/work_ledger.py:520–559`); supersession subsequently enqueues reconcile (`src/rememberstack/workers/e3.py:757–780`). v1 has reached its claims frontier, but not its facts frontier.
   - v2 enters claims, finishes Selection/Claimify and reaches its facts boundary. It cannot enter facts before v1's full reconcile. Section 4.3 also withholds v2's entire claims tail while v1 has fact work in flight. v2 now has an entered claims gap and an unentered facts gap, with no `embed_claim` row.
   - v3 finishes sources and becomes current. Current-version publication is at structure completion, independently of claims/facts completion (`src/rememberstack/spine/document_catalog.py:1186–1227`). Its claims boundary waits for v2's claims frontier. v3 has no facts gap yet because it has not extracted.
   - Resume v1's full reconcile. Its facts-frontier event invokes Advance. Step 2 discharges v2's unentered facts gap because v2 is no longer current. Step 3 finds no current-version facts target and stops. The claims-tail branch in step 6 never runs for v2.

   v2 cannot reach its claims frontier without the missing `embed_claim` (§5.1). v3 cannot enter claims until v2 does. Thus v3 can never reach the facts boundary that might otherwise provide another event. Re-running setup does not repair this: seeding excludes discharged facts gaps, and advancing claims cannot discharge v2's **entered** claims gap. There is no failed work row to replay. If step 2 is interpreted to discharge gaps across both layers, rather than only the layer being advanced, the same failure occurs sooner; the entered claims gap is still retained.

   This is a protocol-level missing transition, not a request for implementation pseudocode. Section 4.3 promises release after the last earlier facts frontier, but the specified Advance procedure loses the very version whose tail needs releasing. The failure also applies if v1 is draining after an off/on switch; it does not require changing any reuse key.

   **Concrete fix:** draining an already-entered claims layer must survive discharge of its facts gap. Before current-only facts target selection/early returns, release eligible pending claims tails for every version that entered claims but will not enter facts, including versions whose facts gap is discharged. Retain the existing earlier-fact-work ordering and deletion rules. Derive this obligation from the existing claims/work-ledger state or preserve an equivalent durable obligation; do not use only an open unentered current-version facts gap to discover it. No polling job or corpus rebuild is necessary. Extend tests 8–10 with the three-version schedule above, asserting both gaps settle, v3 becomes ready, and unchanged Selection/Claimify products and vectors are reused. Also exercise facts off/on while this tail waits.

   Test 9's current pause “v1 entered but not yet normalised” does not reach the important configured steady-ingestion window: the new claims frontier already prevents v2 extraction until v1's `embed_claim` succeeds. Include the reachable pause **after v1 claim embedding and before its full reconcile**, as above, as well as the separate backfill case where claims had completed before facts were enabled.
