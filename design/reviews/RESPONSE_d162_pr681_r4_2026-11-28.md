# Response to PR #681 design review, round 4

Review of `3b337120a`. Accepted all three P1 findings and the D56 wording
note. The review reported no P0 or P2 findings.

## P1

1. **Keep checking inherited neighbour slots after a split disappears.**
   Legacy E1 gives the same empty slots to an absent neighbour and to one
   across a child section. The E2 window query distinguishes them. The
   chunk row carries the comparison requirement into an unsplit grid.
   A marked candidate requires D140 and Selection to check every matching
   window. Claimify checks the marker for each matched target and producer.
   [E1 slots](../../src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py#L734-L765) ·
   [E2 window query](../../src/rememberstack/spine/chunk_catalog.py#L373-L419) ·
   [Reuse rules](../../plan/designs/split_oversized_chunks_design.md#2-split-only-failing-candidates).

2. **Reuse a frozen Selection result at the chosen date.** D140 can pick
   a failed older occurrence when dates tie. Selection looks up committed
   output by key and prefers newer occurrences. The design resolves the
   date first, then gives a verified frozen result at that date priority
   for the slot pairs in both keys. Acceptance requires zero paid Selection
   calls in the tied-date case.
   [D140 order](../../src/rememberstack/core/text_origin.py#L54-L85) ·
   [Selection lookup](../../src/rememberstack/spine/selection_catalog.py#L102-L138) ·
   [Acceptance](../../plan/designs/split_oversized_chunks_design.md#4-acceptance).

3. **Fence operator replay at cutover.** The ledger moves a dead letter
   directly to `pending` and the replayer announces it after commit. The
   design closes that transition during the drain and marks the remaining
   legacy CHUNK and EMBED_CHUNK dead letters ineligible. The orchestration
   contract applies the same rule to single and bulk replay.
   [Ledger transition](../../src/rememberstack/spine/work_ledger.py#L2365-L2392) ·
   [Announcement](../../src/rememberstack/workers/operations.py#L20-L42) ·
   [Replay contract](../../plan/designs/orchestration_design.md#6-dead-letter-operations).
