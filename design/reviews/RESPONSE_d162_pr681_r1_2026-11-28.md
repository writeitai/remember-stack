# Response to PR #681 design review, round 1

Review of `bbcc32a5e`. I accepted the P1, P2 and D80 clarification.

## P1

- **Section-entry IDs.** The blockizer, heading parser and packer produce a whole chunk
  at `[5, 5)` for the `terms` section in `Lead\u2028\n# Terms {#terms}\n`. On a scratch
  PostgreSQL 17 instance, both current lookup queries returned that chunk by ordinal.
  Character containment dropped it. D162 and D140 keep the ordinal rule for whole
  chunks, apply character containment to split pieces and preserve scope and order.
  Acceptance covers the zero-width case in both readers.

  [Document references query](../../src/rememberstack/spine/document_references.py#L1366-L1382) ·

## P2

- **Persisted extraction outputs.** Claimify computes its input stamp from Selection hashes
  before grounding and writing the accepted claims. Frozen replay now requires equality of
  the stored Selection payloads, claim rows and `chunk_claims` links with occurrence spans.
  [Selection writes](../../src/rememberstack/spine/selection_catalog.py#L155-L180) ·

## D80 clarification

- A renderer check confirmed that chunk count can change header mode and renumbering a

  recheck to chunk count. Rule 4 describes vector reuse using the hash of the exact
  rendered string.
