## P0 — Blocking inaccuracies & broken test specification

### 1. `CLAUDE.md:76-79` and `CLAUDE.md:97-101` — Omits D122's 8-chunk cross-section Claimify dependency (`claimify_input_hash`) and conflates embedding, Selection, and Claimify blast radii, making the required test fail as stated

- **File / lines:** `CLAUDE.md:76-79`, `CLAUDE.md:97-101`
- **Problem:**
  1. **Prose (`CLAUDE.md:76-79`):** Claims that *"Unchanged parts reuse their previous chunk keys, embeddings and extracted claims; only the edited chunks, plus at most their immediate neighbours in the same section, are re-chunked, re-embedded and re-extracted. Example: in a 200-section contract, rewording one paragraph of § 12 must not touch § 1–11 or § 13–200."*
  2. **Test requirement (`CLAUDE.md:97-101`):** Requires asserting *"that only that paragraph's chunk and its immediate same-section neighbours get new extractions and embeddings, while every other chunk reuses its claims and vector."*
  3. **What the code actually does:**
     - **E2 extraction is split into two stages with different reuse keys (`src/rememberstack/workers/e2.py:500-605`, D122):**
       - **Selection (`EXTRACT_CLAIMS`)** keys on `extraction_input_hash` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e2.py:532-545, 641-647`), which hashes the chunk's own `chunk_content_hash` and its immediate ±1 **same-section** neighbour hashes (`src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:709-722`). When one chunk's text changes without shifting chunk boundaries, Selection runs on at most **1–3 chunks** (the edited chunk and its immediate same-section neighbours).
       - **Claimify (`GROUND_CLAIMS`)**, by contrast, keys on `claimify_input_hash` (`src/rememberstack/core/selection_references.py:66-82`, called at `src/rememberstack/workers/e2.py:577-591`), which hashes `(target_selection_input_hash, *preceding_selection_input_hashes, REFERENCE_POLICY_VERSION)` where `preceding` (`preceding_producer_ids` in `src/rememberstack/workers/extraction_references.py:54-61` and `chunks_for_references` in `src/rememberstack/spine/chunk_catalog.py:457-465`) spans the **previous 8 source chunks in document-ordinal order (`PRECEDING_CHUNK_LIMIT = 8`), explicitly crossing section boundaries** (`plan/designs/document_reference_context_design.md:51-53`).
       - Consequently, when paragraph edit in chunk $k$ changes `extraction_input_hash` for $\{k-1, k, k+1\}$ (same section), `claimify_input_hash` changes for up to **8 subsequent chunks after the last chunk whose Selection hash changed** (up to ordinal $k + 9$, which spills into § 13+ whenever § 12 has fewer than 8 chunks after the edit). Every chunk in that 8-chunk trailing window that has kept propositions fails `_reuse_prior_claimify` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e2.py:796-804`), re-runs Claimify (`_claimify_chunk`), mints new `claims` rows (which then also run E3 `NormalizeRelationsHandler`), and does **not** reuse prior `claim_id`s. Existing tests explicitly account for this 3-way split (`hash_new + claimify_reused + claimify_rerun == v2_chunk_count` in `src/tests/workers/test_reuse_lifecycle.py:453-461`; see also `src/tests/workers/test_d140_text_origin_pg.py:125-129`).
     - **Embeddings (`EMBED_CHUNK`)** carry forward on `(chunk_content_hash, embedding_text_hash, policy_generation, embedding_version)` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:325-343`) and do **not** include neighbour hashes. When chunk boundaries do not shift, **only the edited chunk itself (1 chunk, not its ±1 neighbours)** gets a new embedding; the immediate neighbours reuse their prior vectors.
- **Concrete fix:**
  - In `CLAUDE.md:75-81`, distinguish the three reuse scopes:
    > "Unchanged parts reuse their previous chunk keys, embeddings, Selection results, and extracted claims: when a single paragraph is edited without shifting chunk boundaries, **only the edited chunk is re-embedded**; **only the edited chunk and its immediate (±1) same-section neighbours re-run E2 Selection** (`extraction_input_hash`); and **only those chunks plus up to the next 8 chunks in document order that have kept propositions re-run E2 Claimify** (`claimify_input_hash`, D122, which looks back 8 chunks across section boundaries for reference cards). Example: in a 200-section contract, rewording one paragraph of § 12 never re-embeds or re-runs Selection in § 1–11 or § 13–200, and never touches Claimify in § 1–11 or beyond the 8-chunk D122 reference window after the edit."
  - In `CLAUDE.md:97-101`, make the required regression test match actual engine invariants (and point to `src/tests/workers/test_reuse_lifecycle.py` and `src/tests/workers/test_d140_text_origin_pg.py`):
    > "- **Prove it with a test.** A change that can affect ingestion must keep (or add) a regression test (see `src/tests/workers/test_reuse_lifecycle.py` and `src/tests/workers/test_d140_text_origin_pg.py`) that ingests v1 of a multi-section document and then v2 with one paragraph reworded, and asserts that: (1) only the edited chunk is re-embedded while every other chunk carries its vector forward; (2) only the edited chunk and its immediate (±1) same-section neighbours get a new `extraction_input_hash` and call E2 Selection; and (3) every chunk outside those Selection-invalidated chunks and D122's trailing 8-chunk Claimify producer window (`PRECEDING_CHUNK_LIMIT = 8`) reuses its prior `claim_id`s via `chunk_claims` with zero new `claims` rows minted."

## P1 — Technical inaccuracies & completeness gaps

### 2. `CLAUDE.md:77-78` and `CLAUDE.md:87-88` — Misstates how section containment vs. content anchors (`D58`, `core/chunker.py`) bound chunk-boundary shifts

- **File / lines:** `CLAUDE.md:77-78`, `CLAUDE.md:87-88`
- **Problem:**
  - Lines 87–88 state: *"Chunk boundaries are anchor-stabilized, whole-block runs (D58), so an edit cannot shift boundaries elsewhere in the document."* Lines 77–78 say *"only the edited chunks, plus at most their immediate neighbours in the same section, are re-chunked"*.
  - In `src/rememberstack/core/chunker.py`, two separate mechanisms bound boundary shifts:
    1. **Between sections:** `pack_blocks` packs each contiguous deepest-owner section run independently (`_owner_runs`, `src/rememberstack/core/chunker.py:93-132`). Chunks never cross section boundaries, which is why an edit inside § 12 cannot shift chunk boundaries in § 1–11 or § 13–200 (provided heading structure outside § 12 is unchanged).
    2. **Within a section run:** `_pack_section` (`src/rememberstack/core/chunker.py:181-238`) packs whole blocks greedily up to `token_budget` (default 400 whitespace tokens) and forces a cut before a block when `is_anchor` holds (`int(block_hash[:16], 16) % anchor_modulus == 0` with default `anchor_modulus = 24`, `chunker.py:47, 176-178`) and `tokens_since_anchor >= anchor_min_gap_tokens` (default 200 tokens, `chunker.py:48, 221-229`), or when extraction eligibility changes (`chunker.py:225-226`).
  - Because anchors occur on average every ~24 blocks (several chunks) rather than after every chunk, an edit in a multi-chunk section that changes block token counts (or flips a block's anchor status) **can shift chunk boundaries for subsequent chunks within that same section run up to the next content anchor (or the end of the section run)**. (Also note: `ChunkHandler` always runs deterministic `pack_blocks` over the new representation's block grid and inserts `chunks` rows for the new version; what unchanged chunks reuse is their `chunk_content_hash`, `reuse_identity_hash`, `text_origin_at`, and `extraction_input_hash`.)
- **Concrete fix:** Replace the last sentence of bullet 1 (`CLAUDE.md:87-88`) with:
  > "Chunks are non-overlapping runs of whole blocks packed independently per deepest-owning section (`_owner_runs`), with boundaries cut at extraction-eligibility transitions (D133) and content-hash anchors (`uint64(block_hash) mod anchor_modulus == 0` after `anchor_min_gap_tokens`, D58): an edit in one section can never shift chunk boundaries in other sections, and within the edited section any packing shift stops at the next content anchor (or the end of the section run)."

### 3. `CLAUDE.md:83-87` — Incomplete specification of extraction reuse keys (`reuse_identity_hash`, `extraction_input_hash`, `claimify_input_hash`) and D80 embedding carry-forward

- **File / lines:** `CLAUDE.md:83-87`
- **Problem:**
  1. **Two-stage D140 identity (`reuse_identity_hash` → `text_origin_at` → `extraction_input_hash`):**
     - `reuse_identity_hash` (`src/rememberstack/core/text_origin.py:22-49`, called at `src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:642-654`) hashes `REUSE_IDENTITY_VERSION` (`"d140-reuse-identity-1"`), `(chunk_content_hash,)`, `(prev_chunk_content_hash, next_chunk_content_hash)` (same `section_id`, `""` if absent), undated `header_facts`, **`blockizer_version`**, `structurer_version`, and `extractor_version` (`E2_EXTRACTOR_VERSION`, which embeds `SECTION_ORIENTATION_VERSION`). This date-free hash is what looks up `text_origin_at` in earlier non-deleted versions of the lineage (`src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:179-203`).
     - `extraction_input_hash` (`src/rememberstack/core/chunker.py:141-168`, `src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:673-697`) then hashes `(chunk_content_hash,)`, `(prev_hash, next_hash)`, dated `header_facts` (`(header_title, source_kind, text_origin_at_iso, language, file_name)`), `extractor_version`, and `structurer_version`.
     - For extraction-ineligible chunks (`not chunk.extraction_eligible`), `_undated_header_facts` also appends `f"ineligible:{EXTRACTION_ELIGIBILITY_POLICY_VERSION}"` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:739-747`), making `EXTRACTION_ELIGIBILITY_POLICY_VERSION` part of both hashes for ineligible chunks.
     - Neither `blockizer_version`, `REUSE_IDENTITY_VERSION`, `EXTRACTION_ELIGIBILITY_POLICY_VERSION`, nor `claimify_input_hash` / `REFERENCE_POLICY_VERSION` (D122) is mentioned in `CLAUDE.md:83-87`.
  2. **Span-remapping requirement (`D119`/`D122`):**
     - Matching `extraction_input_hash` / `claimify_input_hash` is necessary but not sufficient for E2 reuse: both `_remap_prior_selection_cards` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e2.py:650-658`) and `_reuse_prior_claimify` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e2.py:805-812`) call `remap_evidence_spans` (`src/rememberstack/core/source_passages.py:265-305`), which translates each stored evidence span by relative character offset inside the chunk window (`target`/`previous`/`next` or `target-9..target+1`) and verifies exact substring equality (`prior_md[span.char_start:span.char_end] == current_md[new_start:new_end]`). If remapping fails, reuse is refused and the model is called.
  3. **Embedding carry-forward (`D80`):**
     - Line 86–87 states: *"Embeddings carry forward on the content and embedding-input hashes (D80)."*
     - In `src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:267-275, 325-343` and `src/rememberstack/spine/chunk_catalog.py:421-438` (`_SELECT_CARRY_FORWARD`), cross-version vector carry-forward requires matching the 4-tuple **`(chunk_content_hash, embedding_text_hash, policy_generation [EMBEDDING_INPUT_POLICY_VERSION], embedding_version [embedding_model])`** (and an existing vector in P1).
     - Moreover, `embedding_text_hash` is computed over the rendered D80 input (`src/rememberstack/core/embedding_input_policy.py:188-234`), which depends on `LocationFacts` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:614-639`): lineage `title` (`documents.title`), `section_title`, `section_role`, `source_shape`, whether `chunk_count` is `1` vs. `>= 2` (`_decide_mode`, `embedding_input_policy.py:261-273`), and connector message metadata (`channel_ref`, `author_ref`, `message_ts`). Any change to a section's `section_title` or `section_role` (or lineage `title`) changes `embedding_text_hash` and re-embeds every chunk in that section (or document) even when `chunk_content_hash` is unchanged.
- **Concrete fix:** Expand bullet 1 (`CLAUDE.md:83-88`) to name the exact keys and their inputs:
  > "- **Know what the reuse keys contain** (D56, refined by D80, D119, D122, D133, D134, D140):
  >   - *Text-origin identity (`reuse_identity_hash`, D140):* `REUSE_IDENTITY_VERSION`, the chunk's `chunk_content_hash` (sha256 of its ordered `block_hash`es), its previous/next same-section neighbour `chunk_content_hash`es (`""` when absent), the undated header facts (`header_title()`, `source_kind`, `language`, `file_name` — D134 — plus `ineligible:<EXTRACTION_ELIGIBILITY_POLICY_VERSION>` when ineligible, D133), `blockizer_version`, `structurer_version`, and `extractor_version` (which includes `SECTION_ORIENTATION_VERSION`).
  >   - *Selection reuse key (`extraction_input_hash`, D56/D140):* the same own and same-section neighbour `chunk_content_hash`es, the header facts with the chunk's resolved `text_origin_at` ISO timestamp inserted after `source_kind`, `extractor_version`, and `structurer_version`.
  >   - *Claimify reuse key (`claimify_input_hash`, D122):* the chunk's `extraction_input_hash`, the `extraction_input_hash`es of up to 8 preceding chunks in document order (`PRECEDING_CHUNK_LIMIT = 8`), and `REFERENCE_POLICY_VERSION`. Both Selection card reuse and Claimify claim reuse also require exact relative-offset character span remapping (`remap_evidence_spans`, D119) to succeed across windows.
  >   - *Embedding carry-forward key (D80):* `(chunk_content_hash, embedding_text_hash, EMBEDDING_INPUT_POLICY_VERSION, embedding_model)`, where `embedding_text_hash` hashes the D80-rendered location header + normalized body built from `LocationFacts` (lineage `title`, `section_title`, `section_role`, `source_shape`, the `chunk_count == 1` vs `>= 2` header mode threshold, and `channel_ref`/`author_ref`/`message_ts`)."

### 4. `CLAUDE.md:89-104` — Completeness: silent reuse killers not named in Rule 4

- **File / lines:** `CLAUDE.md:89-104`
- **Problem:** Beyond version-specific text in chunks/headers and converter non-determinism, five mechanisms in the current codebase can silently cause 100% or multi-section reprocessing on a new version if an implementer or caller does not know about them:
  1. **Lineage identity (`(source_kind, source_ref)`) vs. keyless one-shot `upload` (`src/rememberstack/workers/e0.py:218-367`):** Every reuse query (`_SELECT_TEXT_ORIGIN_MATCHES`, `_SELECT_CARRY_FORWARD`, `selections.prior`, `prior_extracted_chunk`) is scoped to `doc_id`. `UploadIngestor.ingest` (one-shot upload without `source_kind`/`source_ref`) sets `source_ref = content_hash` and derives a new `doc_id` per byte payload (`e0.py:251-253`), yielding **0% reuse**. Version reuse requires ingesting into the same lineage via stable `(source_kind, source_ref)` (`ingest_observed`).
  2. **Dropping, adding, or back-filling `source_modified_at` / `published_at` across versions (`src/rememberstack/core/text_origin.py:52-83`, `src/rememberstack/spine/chunk_catalog.py:331-345`):**
     - If `v1` had a `version_date` (`source_modified_at or published_at`) and `v2` is ingested with `version_date = None`, `earliest_eligible_match` returns `None` (`text_origin.py:63-64`), setting `text_origin_at = None` and changing `extraction_input_hash` for **every chunk** (`src/tests/workers/test_d140_text_origin_pg.py:219-233`).
     - Conversely, if `v1` had `version_date = None` (`text_origin_at IS NULL`) and `v2` supplies a date, `_SELECT_TEXT_ORIGIN_MATCHES` filters `c.text_origin_at IS NOT NULL` (`chunk_catalog.py:340`), so `v2` matches nothing from `v1` and re-extracts 100% of the document.
     - If an older-dated version is ingested *after* a newer-dated version (`version_date < prior.text_origin_at`), `c.text_origin_at <= :not_after` (`chunk_catalog.py:341`) intentionally refuses inheritance and re-extracts the older edition in full (`test_d140_text_origin_pg.py:168-193`).
  3. **Intra-block whitespace / line-wrap drift breaking D119/D122 span remapping (`src/rememberstack/core/source_passages.py:265-305`):**
     - `block_hash` normalizes line breaks and collapses internal whitespace (`normalized_block_text` in `src/rememberstack/core/blockizer.py:86-102`), so reflowing lines inside an unchanged paragraph preserves `block_hash`, `chunk_content_hash`, and `extraction_input_hash`. However, `remap_evidence_spans` translates `char_start - prior_window[0]` by raw character offset in `document.md` and checks `prior_md[span.char_start:span.char_end] == current_md[new_start:new_end]` (`source_passages.py:293-299`). Intra-block whitespace drift therefore passes hash checks but **silently fails span remapping (`None`)**, forcing Selection and Claimify to re-run!
  4. **E0 fallback structuring and LLM title-role classification (`src/rememberstack/workers/e0.py:960-1067, 1243-1308, 1404-1453`):**
     - When a document lacks sufficient Markdown headings (or fails the heading-density, oversized-leaf, or skeleton-check gates), E0 invokes the fallback structure LLM (`_fallback`) to propose section anchors. If the fallback model proposes different section boundaries on v2, `_owner_runs` and `same_section_neighbours` change across the document.
     - When section titles are not matched by `deterministic_section_role`, E0 calls the title-only LLM classifier (`_assign_roles`). If a section's classified `role` changes on v2, `LocationFacts.section_role` changes and invalidates `embedding_text_hash` for every chunk in that section.
  5. **E0 section-summary cache invalidation when block ordinals or `node_path` shift (`src/rememberstack/workers/e0_summary.py:761-816`):**
     - `SectionSummarizer` caches section summaries across versions of a `doc_id`, but `_summary_cache_key` hashes `_render_block` — which embeds `block.ordinal` (`f"[block ordinal={block.ordinal} ...]"`, `e0_summary.py:813`) — and `section.node_path` (`e0_summary.py:778`). Adding or removing even a single paragraph block in § 12 shifts `block.ordinal` for every block in § 13–200, invalidating the E0 section-summary cache for every later section (causing E0 flash summary calls to re-run for § 13–200, even though E1/E2 reuse keys stay intact because summaries are excluded from `extraction_input_hash` and `embedding_text_hash`).
- **Concrete fix:** Add these pitfalls explicitly to bullets 2 and 5 (`CLAUDE.md:89-92, 102-104`):
  - In bullet 2 (`CLAUDE.md:89-92`), add:
    > "Keep `source_modified_at`/`published_at` presence consistent across versions of a lineage (switching between dated and undated versions, or ingesting an older-dated edition after a newer-dated one, prevents `text_origin_at` inheritance under D140 and re-extracts the whole version)."
  - In bullet 5 (`CLAUDE.md:102-104`), add:
    > "Connectors must ingest successive versions under the same `(source_kind, source_ref)` lineage (keyless one-shot `upload` derives `doc_id` from `content_hash` and never reuses across edits), emit canonical Markdown headings so E0 stays on the deterministic heading parser rather than the LLM anchor fallback, and preserve exact intra-block whitespace (because `remap_evidence_spans` translates raw character offsets inside each chunk window even when normalized `block_hash`es match). Also note that E0's section-summary cache (`_summary_cache_key` in `workers/e0_summary.py`) currently hashes `block.ordinal` and `section.node_path`, so block insertions/deletions invalidate E0 summary cache entries for later sections even though E1/E2 chunk reuse keys are unaffected."
