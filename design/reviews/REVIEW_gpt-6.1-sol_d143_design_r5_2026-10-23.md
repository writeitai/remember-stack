1. **P1 must fix — Claims-only reconciliation can overlap its own version’s admitted facts work and retire testimony before E3 applies it.** Location: `plan/designs/processing_layers_design.md:323–329` (§5.2 step 0), `:346–350` (facts admission), `:199–210` (§4.2); compare §4.3.

   Step 0 defines an owed tail as occurrences complete, an unentered facts gap, and no existing tail rows. Those conditions also hold for the current target that this same invocation will immediately admit to facts. It therefore opens `embed_claim` and the claims-mode reconcile before step 7 opens E3. The earlier-fact-work condition does not prevent this: it examines earlier versions, not the version receiving both paths. This contradicts §4.2’s condition that the claims tail is opened when facts are **not** entered, and bypasses the ordering §4.3 relies on.

   A concrete schedule in a configured deployment with both layers enabled:

   - v1 reaches its facts boundary with claim C. With no unfinished predecessor, step 0 opens its claims tail and step 7 admits its facts work. Pause v1’s E3 and claims-mode reconcile; finish its early `embed_claim`.
   - v2 becomes current, enters claims after v1’s claims frontier, and finishes its occurrences, omitting C. This current-version publication is independent of E3 completion (`src/rememberstack/spine/document_catalog.py:1186–1227`). v2’s own tail correctly waits for v1’s facts frontier.
   - Run **v1’s** claims-mode reconcile. The §4.2 guard passes because current v2’s occurrences are complete. Reconcile retires C against v2 before v1 has built C’s fact.
   - Resume v1’s E3. It normalizes and applies C despite its retired currency. Its subsequent full reconcile has no new retirement transition to settle the newly created fact.

   The last step is observable in the actual code, not hypothetical handler behavior: normalize validates occurrence membership and calls `_normalize_claim` without a currency gate (`src/rememberstack/workers/e3.py:255–299`). Application can create a fact from that assertion (`src/rememberstack/spine/apply_fact_decision.py:101–146`); recount sets its current support to zero but does not close it (`:414–427`). Reconcile discovers only current stale testimony, recovers transitions only under its **own** processing/reconciliation id, and scopes recount and closure to those transitions (`src/rememberstack/workers/reconcile.py:106–188`; `src/rememberstack/spine/lifecycle.py:932–945`, `:1033–1041`). The claims-mode run’s retirement preceded the fact and used a different id. An open-window fact can remain unclosed with zero support. This recreates the retired-testimony failure that round 3’s ordering was intended to eliminate.

   There is also an off/on variant even if step 0 is interpreted narrowly using §4.2: with facts off, v1 legitimately opens its claims tail; let `embed_claim` succeed but leave its claims-mode reconcile pending. Enable facts and admit current v1, then allow v2 to finish occurrences while v1’s E3 is paused. The already-queued v1 claims-mode reconcile can now retire C exactly as above. Facts admission does not wait for or otherwise order this existing tail. Checking only whether an earlier version has fact work in flight does not cover either schedule.

   **Concrete fix:** make claims-only tail selection mutually exclusive with immediate facts admission, while retaining round 4’s whole-lineage release before early returns for versions that really need that tail, including discharged facts gaps. Also define the ordering when a version with an existing claims-mode reconcile subsequently enters facts: its currency-changing reconciliation must not be able to run against a newer current version ahead of its own admitted E3 work. For example, settle the existing claims-mode reconcile before facts admission, with a guaranteed completion wake-up for the deferred facts gap. Preserve the ordinary post-adjudication `embed_claim`/full-reconcile path for versions admitted directly to facts (`work_ledger.py:520–559`; `workers/e3.py:757–780`). No currency-filter/suppression protocol or reuse-key change is needed. Extend test 9 with both schedules, using an open-window fact, and assert final support/closure, frontier completion, and unchanged provider-call reuse.

2. **P1 must fix — Discharging a deferred claims gap can leave a permanent gapless blocker in the facts layer.** Location: `plan/designs/processing_layers_design.md:161–169` (§4.1), `:331–342` (§5.2 steps 2 and 5), `:361–364` (§5.3).

   A disabled claims boundary explicitly records **both** claims and facts gaps. An enabled claims boundary instead records the gap for the layer being entered, and can defer that version behind a predecessor. If a newer living version becomes current, advance discharges the deferred version’s unentered **claims** gap. That version will never extract and therefore never reach its facts boundary; it has neither a facts frontier nor a discharged facts gap. Facts advance nevertheless requires every earlier sources-complete live version to have one of those two proofs.

   A concrete three-version living lineage, with claims and facts enabled in a configured deployment:

   - v0 has entered claims; pause its `embed_claim` so it has not reached the claims frontier. Its facts work can already have completed.
   - v1 completes `embed_chunk`. Its enabled claims boundary records an unentered claims gap and waits behind v0. It records no facts gap: facts are not disabled at this boundary, and v1 has not reached the extract barrier.
   - v2 becomes current and completes sources. Claims advance discharges v1’s unentered claims gap. Once v0 reaches its claims frontier, v2 enters claims, bypassing v1’s discharged claims gap as intended.
   - At v2’s facts boundary, facts advance step 5 sees sources-complete v1 with no full reconcile and no discharged **facts** gap, and stops forever. v1 cannot produce the promised frontier wake-up because its claims work was deliberately never enqueued. Step 0 cannot manufacture the missing facts disposition, and repeated seeding cannot change the predicate.

   The actual chain confirms why no later facts event can arrive from v1: `_extract_follow_up` opens the per-chunk extraction jobs (`src/rememberstack/workers/e1.py:772–830`), and `complete_chunk_extract` opens E3 only after the completed extract barrier (`src/rememberstack/spine/work_ledger.py:315–336`). Discharging before extraction removes that route entirely. This is not a dead letter that an operator can replay. The lineage remains backfilling/unready until the live v1 is deleted. It also undermines the explicit promise that every deferred lineage completes without another setup run.

   **Concrete fix:** define how discharging an unentered lower-layer gap proves that every dependent layer was also deliberately skipped. Either record and discharge the corresponding facts gap under the same boundary/lineage protocol, or let facts ordering explicitly recognize a discharged unentered claims gap as its dependent-layer discharge proof. Carry that rule through coverage/readiness and deletion repointing: when v1 becomes current again, both missing layers must become eligible in dependency order, with reliable wake-ups. Preserve the existing chunks, text origins and embedding vectors; do not re-run sources or weaken checks for genuinely entered/dead-lettered predecessors. Add this three-version schedule to test 8 and its delete-current/repoint variant to test 7. This is a missing cross-layer terminal disposition, not a request for implementation pseudocode.
