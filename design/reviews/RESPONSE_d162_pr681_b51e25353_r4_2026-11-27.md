and use distinct relation and observation application versions. The
existing fan-out guard looks up state by version and normalizer. D90 now
keys state and entity units by the grid and pinned composed generation.

The fact-dependent follow-ups also get distinct delivery keys when another
generation is admitted on the same grid.
