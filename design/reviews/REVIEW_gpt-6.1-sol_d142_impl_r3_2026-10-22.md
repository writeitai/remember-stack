## Judgements

**Not all other findings are closed sufficiently to approve immediately after the P1-6 decision.** The engine repairs are real, but the SQL bridge still needs the budget-error classification repair in R3 P2-1. This is the remaining lower-severity portion of R2 P1-3, not a new latency finding. The proof/documentation nits below are nonblocking.

**P1-6 remains the owner's existing decision; it is not re-raised here.** The final 300k archive is materially better evidence: public results hydrate, all 40 current/history queries return k, no measured stratum stops early, and unfiltered p95 is **263.2/266.8 ms**. That supports the approximately 300 ms budget for these measured unfiltered cases at this size. Filtered p95 is still **351 ms** at 600 eligible rows, **859 ms** at 6,000, and **2,084 ms** for the 4,990-row exact tier. The 5,010-row pushdown case takes **780 ms**, with the same 35/40 returned-k rate. That strongly argues that the document exact-tier threshold is too high; it does not by itself identify a replacement threshold or prove all of the cost comes from scoring.

An honest amended acceptance criterion must explicitly replace the blanket interactive latency promise with named **size- and stratum-specific budgets**, including absolute eligible-row counts as well as document fractions. It must state the public operation/channel, corpus/version/duplication shape, machine and cache conditions, query population and repetitions, and pair latency with returned-k/completeness and early-stop rates. A lower returned-k rate where fewer positive lexical matches exist is different from an early stop. If common-stratum truncation becomes acceptable, its allowed rate and typed disclosure must be explicit. The amendment must say that multi-second filtered reads are accepted where that is the decision; it cannot call these measurements a 300 ms pass or extrapolate the 300k result to 3M/50M. The older nonhydrating 3M public data remains diagnostic. Reconcile the binding design, D142, delivery plan, analysis and public claims together. Either the measured immutable-doc_id route or that explicit reviewed amendment remains a legitimate resolution of P1-6.

**BM25 refusal:** the corrected proof permits at most **4,194,288 analyzed terms**. Beyond that, `score_epsilon` raises a plain `ValueError`, reached when the loop evaluates its fill bound; it is not a typed search result, and drained/exact paths do not invoke it. Ordinary 4,096-character query requests and the sandbox's SQL/parameter caps are far below this domain boundary. I found no ordinary-query regression from the refusal. The internal guard's public wording can be tightened as Nit 4.

**Owner invariant:** no change to chunking, reuse keys, extractor/Claimify input construction, embedding inputs, text-origin selection or carry-forward appears in `origin/main...HEAD`. The production changes near ingestion are vector acceptance checks, not identity changes. The strengthened three-section regression is useful: it traces actual Selection/Claimify targets, checks origin/reuse identities, confines the known Claimify spillover and keeps unrelated expectations outside the xfail. It does not claim to fix the pre-existing D122 or packing gaps. Nit 3 would make its exact-call-count claim precise.

**Merge:** origin/main is an ancestor of HEAD; AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md match it. The merge's first-parent changes are the agreement and Rule 4 review documents; the remerge diff reports no conflict resolutions. No implementation conflict regression was found.
