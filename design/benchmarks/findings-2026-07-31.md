# Findings: first full publication run (2026-07-30/31)

Analysis, not binding. Protocol `RS-LoCoMo-Full-v5-strong` (luna answer
agent at effort `none`, luna judge, k=10 claims-only retrieval, 6-word
answer cap), engine at extractor `07j` (token-tolerant grounding + temporal
anchoring incl. quoted forms). Run split: conversations 26/30/41/42/43
sequential on the benchmark host, 44+47 / 48 / 49+50 on three shard hosts.
Combined score computed as the sum of the four per-run official summaries
(exact; see runbook §6 for why).

## Headline

**517 / 1540 correct (33.6%), official F1 0.3054.**
Evaluator cost $5.28, ingestion ~$7. Reader retries fired 83× (the retry
mechanism is not optional). Zero unrecovered mechanical failures.

## Context for the number

Published LoCoMo results are not comparable across systems: mem0's paper
reports ~67% under its own protocol; 90%+ figures circulating publicly are
full-context baselines (the model reads the whole transcript) or lenient
judging. Our protocol is intentionally strict — claims-only retrieval, no
transcript access, six-word answers, strict judge. That context does not
make 33.6% good; it makes the gap *diagnosable* rather than mysterious.

## Where misses die (miss taxonomy, 660-question sample of the misses)

Deterministic classification of judged misses, cross-checked against
answer traces and the extraction decision ledger:

1. **The agent answers "Unknown" in ~60% of misses.** It is not
   hallucinating wrong answers; the evidence never reached it. This is the
   single most important fact in the run.
2. **In ~33% of misses, not one of the k=10 retrieved claims came from the
   session containing the gold evidence.** Retrieval reach — one embedding
   query, ten verbatim claims — is the bottleneck, not answer synthesis.
   Session-grain recall of the *store* is ~0.69, so the memory usually
   holds more than the per-question retrieval delivers.
3. **Extraction gates cost real questions but are not dominant.** Ledger
   examples: `selection_drop reason=generic` killed "collects sneakers…"
   and the "self-care is important" realization (issue #177);
   `selection_drop reason=opinion` killed feeling/endorsement facts. The
   token-tolerant grounding fix (#180) already collapsed grounding
   rejections 144 → 18 per conversation.
4. **Judge strictness is a visible slice**: "Caroline is transgender." vs
   gold "Transgender woman" judged wrong; "August 2023" vs "early August,
   2023" judged wrong. Real losses, but fixing them changes the score, not
   the memory.
5. **Category 3 is a policy collision, not a capability gap**: LoCoMo
   expects speculative inference ("Would X enjoy…? — Likely no"); our agent
   honestly refuses without direct evidence. A deliberate decision is
   needed about whether to teach the protocol's expected epistemics.
6. Category 1 (19%) additionally reflects that the agent never uses the
   graph/relationship recipes available in its tool catalog — multi-hop
   chains across sessions cannot be assembled from independent
   claims_verbatim queries.

Caveat: the automated classifier's turn-matching is crude (token overlap
against gold-evidence turns); its "extracted vs never-extracted" split was
not reliable enough to publish. The four store-level conclusions above are
supported by direct trace and ledger inspection, not only the classifier.

## What the fix chain bought (measured on conv-26 across engine versions)

| Metric | 07f (before) | 07j (now) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claims extracted | 82 | ~407 |
| Grounding rejections | 144 | 18 |
| Claims with valid-time | 12 (none gold) | 134/407, incl. both gold WHEN facts |
| Smoke score | 1/8 | 4/8 |

Temporal questions went from structurally impossible (relative dates never
anchored) to the second-strongest F1 category in one day of ledger-driven
fixes (#178 reader retry, #179 temporal anchoring, #180 token-tolerant
grounding, #181 quoted-form temporal, #189 follow-up).
