# Support

<AppliesTo products={["remember.dev"]} />

There are two ways to reach remember.dev support: email, and support
records in the console.

## Email

Write to **support@remember.dev**. Use it for anything: a project that
does not come back after a top-up, a cap you need raised, a refund, a
second project slot, closing a project or your account.

Other addresses:

- **privacy@remember.dev** for personal-data requests under the GDPR;
- **security@remember.dev** to report a security problem.

## Support records in the console

Under **Support** in the console, any member of the organisation can open a
support record with a subject and details, and see the organisation's
records with their status and correlation reference.

A record is kept with your organisation. Opening one does not send anyone
a notification yet, so email support@remember.dev as well and mention the
record's subject.

Through the API a record also takes a `category` and a
`correlation_ref` of up to 128 characters; see
[the remember.dev API](/docs/reference/cloud-api#support).

## What to include

Problems are traced by identifiers, not by descriptions. Include:

- your organisation ID and the project or deployment ID;
- the deployment hostname, if the problem is with memory requests;
- the time of the failure, with its time zone;
- the request: method and path, for example `POST /ingest`;
- the full error response body and status code;
- for a document, the `version_id` from the ingest response;
- for a token problem, the token's label or ID, never the secret.

Never send a token secret, a password or a document you would not want
stored in a support system.

## What support can do

- Raise or remove the organisation's monthly cap.
- Raise the number of project slots.
- Cancel a project closure during its cancel window.
- Refund unused credit.
- Remove a member from an organisation or change their role.
- Delete your account.
- Look at the account records for your organisation.

## What support cannot do

- **Recover deleted or lost data.** There are no backups to restore from.
- **Export your memory.** remember.dev does not offer an export.
- **Look inside your deployment on your say-so.** There is no way yet for
  you to grant support time-limited access to your deployment.
- **Promise a response time.** There is no service level agreement.

## Before you write

Many problems show up in places you can check yourself:

- **Billing** shows the balance, the cap and auto top-up.
- **Projects** shows each project's activation and deployment state; a
  `blocked` deployment usually means it could not be funded for the day.
  See [Spend caps and auto top-up](/docs/cloud/spend-controls).
- Your deployment's `POST /readiness` says whether a document is still
  processing or has failed.
- [Limits](/docs/cloud/limits) lists the errors remember.dev returns and why.
