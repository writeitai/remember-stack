# Our mission

<Lead>
  Centralize all your information in a single place. Expose that information
  to AI agents in the best possible way.
</Lead>

Your information is scattered across a life or an organization — email, Slack, documents, meeting notes, agent traces, and more. What was true a month ago may not be true now. People, events, and ideas come and go.

**RememberStack structures that information so AI agents can answer fast and well.** It knows what is true now and what was true a month ago. It accounts for what you know about a person, an event, or a company.

**RememberStack** is Apache-2.0 open-source memory infrastructure for AI agents. This repository is the complete engine.
