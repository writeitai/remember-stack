## Executive Summary

The runtime diff `b575c556..2c547937` implements the complete two-phase extraction architecture (D122) and contextual fact nomination (D123) without introducing speculative machinery, third model calls, new event tables, per-fact event columns, or date windows.

The implementation cleanly splits E2 across existing pipeline stages:
1. `EXTRACT_CLAIMS` executes Selection only: identifies propositions and source-backed referents, saves the full validated response once into `selection_results`, and fences publication against concurrent hard-forget.
2. `GROUND_CLAIMS` runs Claimify after a transactional representation barrier confirms that all chunk Selection results exist. It bounds preceding referents to whole cards within the previous 8 chunks, caps formatted card text at 4,096 characters, and completes zero-keep chunks deterministically with zero model calls.
3. Version reuse enforces negative dependencies by including the ordered Selection input hashes of all preceding 8 chunk producers (including zero-card producers) in `claimify_input_hash`.
4. Contextual fact nomination (D123) preserves the existing same-subject 20-fact baseline, adds up to 8 extra facts sharing current positive testimony context, excludes canonical subject closures, and includes all bindings, memberships, and reverse closures in the full internal snapshot fingerprint.
5. Lifecycle cycle finalization and pipeline readiness derive status from both `EXTRACT_CLAIMS` and `GROUND_CLAIMS`, preventing premature pipeline completion.

## Architectural & Invariant Analysis

### 1. Frozen Selection & Representation Barrier
- Selection results are stored in `selection_results` via [`SelectionCatalog.freeze`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/selection_catalog.py#L146-L208).
- First-writer-wins is guaranteed via PostgreSQL `ON CONFLICT (deployment_id, chunk_id, extractor_version) DO NOTHING`, followed by re-reading the committed row and asserting identical `selection_input_hash`.
- Zero-keep Selection results successfully publish their cards for use by other chunks and complete Claimify with 0 model calls.
- In [`WorkLedger.complete_chunk_selection`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/work_ledger.py#L255-L330), representation advisory locks ensure that `_SELECTION_BARRIER_MISSING` evaluates atomically, transitioning chunks to `PipelineStage.GROUND_CLAIMS` only once all representations' chunk selections are frozen.

### 2. Multi-Span Version Reuse & Negative Dependencies
- [`claimify_input_hash`](../../src/rememberstack/core/selection_references.py#L66-L84) hashes:
  1. `target_selection_input_hash`
  2. All preceding Selection input hashes (ordered by chunk ordinal) across the 8-chunk window, including chunks that yielded zero cards
  3. `policy_version`
- This ensures that editing an earlier chunk—even one that previously emitted no cards—changes the input hash and correctly invalidates Claimify reuse.
- Offset remapping for reused occurrence evidence ranges uses fixed relative slots `target - 9 .. target + 1` ([`reference_windows`](../../src/rememberstack/workers/extraction_references.py#L38-L52)), ensuring that preceding cards that cited their previous neighbor remain unambiguously mappable.

### 3. Privacy, Hard-Forget, and Replay Guards
- All publication paths ([`SelectionCatalog.freeze`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/selection_catalog.py#L169), [`ClaimCatalog.record_extraction`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/claim_catalog.py#L318), [`ClaimCatalog.attach_reused_claims`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/claim_catalog.py#L140), and [`WorkLedger.complete_chunk_selection`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/work_ledger.py#L274)) invoke [`require_extraction_sources_on`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/selection_catalog.py#L33-L62).
- This acquires `pg_advisory_xact_lock_shared` on `hard-forget:{deployment_id}`, checks for active forget operations, and locks documents and representations `FOR SHARE`.
- `_POSTGRES_SCRUB` in [`forget.py`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/forget.py#L1188) deletes from `selection_results` and cascades `application_context_bindings`.
- `_VERIFY_POSTGRES_SCRUB` validates that neither `selection_results` nor `application_context_bindings` retain rows for forgotten sources.

### 4. Contextual Fact Nomination Semantics (D123)
- Normalizer captures up to 4 context references per assertion ([`attempted_context_refs`](../../src/rememberstack/core/context_references.py#L20-L33)). Extra references are recorded as truncated on the frozen output and do not fail the assertion.
- Context references are resolved through the standard resolver and staged atomically with the application and version rows in [`FactApplicationCatalog.stage`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/fact_applications.py#L254-L352).
- Staging verifies a live resolver decision for the claim and entity (`superseded_by IS NULL` and mention matches claim).
- Candidate nomination in [`application_snapshot`](../../src/rememberstack/spine/fact_application_inputs.py#L175-L235):
  - Retains the exact 20-fact baseline.
  - Queries up to 8 additional candidates whose supporting applications share context, explicitly filtering `NOT f.{id_column} = ANY(baseline_ids)`.
  - Enforces positive current testimony (`support_stance = 'supports'` and `is_current_testimony = true`).
  - Excludes the canonical subject closure from matching context.
  - Merges into at most 28 candidates, ordering shared-context candidates first for concise model presentation.
- Snapshot validation recalculates candidate nomination under row locks; changes raise `ApplicationInputChanged`.
