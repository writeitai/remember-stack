1"""Make every ``memory_v1`` association bind its complete coordinates. 

2 

3Batch A correctly made the public query space fail closed on ordinary 

4tombstones, but several logical associations authenticated only the identifier 

5someone expected to be sufficient. Because the large transcript/evidence 

6tables deliberately use logical foreign keys, inconsistent rows can exist and 

7must be omitted rather than borrowing a live lineage from a sibling row. 

8 

9This correction does three structural things: 

10 

11* one cycle-safe ``v_graph_survivor`` becomes the merge-resolution authority; 

12 the query-space helper is only its deployment-labelled adapter; 

13* one private fact-catalog helper owns fact membership, so facts and their 

14 evidence bridge cannot disagree about whether a fact exists; 

15* every corrected content/testimony/identity join binds all applicable 

16 lineage, version, representation, and section coordinates. 

17 

18The original ``p9_01_0022`` migration remains immutable. These 

19``CREATE OR REPLACE`` statements are also consumed by the offline manifest 

20builder, so the corrected definitions and all authorization helpers roll the 

21surface hash without requiring a running PostgreSQL server. 

22"""
