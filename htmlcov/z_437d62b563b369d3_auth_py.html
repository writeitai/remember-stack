13class PerimeterScope(StrEnum): 

14 """What an authenticated caller may do, as a closed vocabulary. 

15 

16 Three values, deliberately. ``INGEST`` exists for D62: a browser that may 

17 add one document must not also be able to create a connector — standing 

18 configuration that keeps pulling from a third-party system after the tab 

19 closes. A scope vocabulary that grows by string concatenation fails open 

20 the first time somebody adds a route, and the perimeter is not the place 

21 to build a permission language: it exists to answer one narrow question 

22 cheaply, in front of every request. 

23 

24 ``WRITE`` includes everything. ``READ`` and ``INGEST`` are disjoint: an 

25 ingest credential may reach only the one ingest route, not retrieval or 

26 any other mutation. 

27 """

40class CredentialKind(StrEnum): 

41 """Which kind of credential authenticated this request (D60). 

42 

43 Audit needs it, and audit alone: the canonical actor id in a D8 event is 

44 the kind's marker followed by the credential id — ``dpcred:<jti>`` for a 

45 deployment token, ``browsercred:<jti>`` for a browser credential — so 

46 "which credential did this" and "which person did this" stay separate 

47 questions with separate answers. 

48 

49 It decides nothing about authority. ``scope`` does that, and a reader who 

50 finds this value used in an authorisation branch has found a bug. 

51 """
