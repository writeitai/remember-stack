1"""D159 forget records: hide at once, sweep until clean. 

2 

3``forget_manifests`` (D74) becomes ``forgets``: one immutable, content-free 

4record per forgotten lineage, and the only forget state. It holds the 

5document's ID, its version IDs, content hashes and source-identity hash, the 

6content-free IDs a sweep collects, and the sweep's visible progress. The 

7``hard_forget`` stage becomes ``forget`` and its work item targets the record 

8(``processing_target`` gains ``forget``). 

9 

10Upgrade: a complete D74 forget becomes a ``complete`` record. An unfinished 

11one (``preparing`` or ``accepted``) becomes ``erasing`` with the hide applied 

12(the lineage and its versions tombstoned) and a forget job enqueued; the job 

13finishes the lineage cascade before its first sweep. Old ``hard_forget`` work 

14rows still queued are skipped. Metering receipts lose their foreign key to the 

15version, so a sweep can delete the version while the receipt keeps its counts 

16and the version's UUID as billing metadata. 

17"""
