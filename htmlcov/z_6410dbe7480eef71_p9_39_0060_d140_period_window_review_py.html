1"""D140 WP-ET.7: in-force periods as an adjudication input, and window review. 

2 

3Three schema objects: 

4 

5- ``relations.period_basis`` and ``observations.period_basis``: a fingerprint 

6 of the periodised evidence claims of a fact and their in-force periods (the 

7 periods of the versions those claims occur in) at the moment the fact's window 

8 was last adjudicated. NULL means that evidence contained no periodised claim. 

9 A fact whose current fingerprint differs from the stored one is reviewed again 

10 (D140 §8.2); the fingerprint is how that review is found and how a repeated 

11 trigger becomes a no-op. 

12- the ``review_fact_windows`` pipeline stage: one job per lineage whose 

13 periods or testimony changed (it finds the affected facts) and one job per 

14 affected fact (it asks the adjudicator whether the fact's window still fits). 

15- ``fact_window_review_attempts``: one claimed review per fact and input 

16 fingerprint, with the model's completed answer once it exists, so a retry 

17 after a failed apply or a duplicate job reuses the answer instead of paying 

18 for another call. A row is deleted when its answer is applied; hard forget 

19 deletes the rows of the facts it touches. 

20 

21All are additive. The downgrade drops the table and columns; PostgreSQL cannot 

22remove an enum value in place, so the stage value stays. 

23"""
