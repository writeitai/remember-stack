1"""D143: processing layers that a deployment can switch off. 

2 

3Two tables: 

4 

5- ``deployment_processing_layers``: one row per deployment once ``setup`` has 

6 written a non-default layer configuration. No row means both the claims and 

7 the facts layer are enabled, so every existing deployment keeps today's 

8 behaviour without a data migration. The check keeps facts impossible without 

9 claims. 

10- ``processing_layer_gaps``: one row per live version and layer that has not 

11 entered the layer yet. Boundaries write it; the lineage-ordered advance 

12 stamps ``entered_at``; a living lineage's advance stamps ``discharged_at`` on 

13 superseded versions; reaching the layer's frontier deletes it. Coverage is 

14 derived from it and never stored. Like ``obs_flush_version_state`` it carries 

15 no foreign key: deletion and hard forget delete the rows explicitly. 

16 

17It also adds ``connector_sync_cycles.layers_reopened_at``: entering a layer 

18for a version whose connector cycle already finalized reopens that cycle, so 

19its zero-support closure is evaluated again once the resumed work is done; the 

20stamp gives the repeated finalization its own reconciliation id. 

21 

22All of it is additive; the downgrade drops it. 

23"""
