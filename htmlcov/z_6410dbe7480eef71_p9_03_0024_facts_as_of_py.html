1"""Create ``memory_v1.facts_as_of``, the bounded bitemporal SRF (design §3.4). 

2 

3The grammar already admits ``facts_as_of`` as a public set-returning function, 

4so until it exists a legal statement fails for a reason the caller cannot act 

5on. It is pure SQL: unlike the semantic functions it needs no projection, so it 

6is a real PostgreSQL function rather than an executor-resolved bridge, and the 

7planner sees straight through it. 

8 

9Both clocks are applied as half-open intervals, which is the D41 rule for 

10facts: a fact holds at ``valid_at`` when ``valid_from <= valid_at`` and either 

11it never stopped holding or it stopped strictly after that instant, and the 

12system believed it at ``believed_at`` under the same rule on the belief axis. 

13The evidence and contradiction counts, and the support state, are the current 

14ones the view already computes under D54 — hence ``identity_regime`` of 

15``current``: the row is the current system's reading of an earlier instant, 

16not a reconstruction of what the counts were then. 

17 

18The row bound is clamped in the function, not trusted from the caller, so the 

19§4.3 hard cap holds however the function is invoked, and the ordering is 

20deterministic so that a bound means the same thing on every run. 

21"""

34CREATE FUNCTION memory_v1.facts_as_of( 

35 valid_at timestamptz, 

36 believed_at timestamptz, 

37 max_rows integer DEFAULT {_MAX_ROWS_DEFAULT} 

38)

40 deployment_id uuid, 

41 fact_kind text, 

42 fact_id uuid, 

43 subject_entity_id uuid, 

44 predicate text, 

45 object_entity_id uuid, 

46 statement text, 

47 fact_label text, 

48 valid_from timestamptz,

50 ingested_at timestamptz, 

51 invalidated_at timestamptz, 

52 contradiction_group uuid, 

53 confidence real, 

54 evidence_count_current bigint, 

55 contradict_count_current bigint, 

56 support_state_current text, 

57 applied_valid_at timestamptz, 

58 applied_believed_at timestamptz, 

59 identity_regime text 

60) 

61LANGUAGE sql 

62STABLE 

63PARALLEL SAFE 

64SECURITY INVOKER 

65SET search_path = memory_v1, pg_catalog 

66AS $$ 

67 SELECT 

68 f.deployment_id, 

69 f.fact_kind, 

70 f.fact_id, 

71 f.subject_entity_id, 

72 f.predicate, 

73 f.object_entity_id, 

74 f.statement, 

75 f.fact_label, 

76 f.valid_from, 

77 f.valid_until, 

78 f.ingested_at, 

79 f.invalidated_at, 

80 f.contradiction_group, 

81 f.confidence, 

82 f.evidence_count_current, 

83 f.contradict_count_current, 

84 f.support_state_current, 

85 facts_as_of.valid_at, 

86 facts_as_of.believed_at, 

87 'current'::text 

88 FROM memory_v1.facts_visible_history AS f 

89 -- A null endpoint is an OPEN interval, not a comparison that fails: a fact 

90 -- with no recorded start held for as long as anyone knows, and treating that 

91 -- as "started after every instant" would hide it from every as-of question. 

92 WHERE (f.ingested_at IS NULL OR f.ingested_at <= facts_as_of.believed_at) 

93 AND (f.invalidated_at IS NULL OR f.invalidated_at > facts_as_of.believed_at) 

94 AND (f.valid_from IS NULL OR f.valid_from <= facts_as_of.valid_at) 

95 AND (f.valid_until IS NULL OR f.valid_until > facts_as_of.valid_at) 

96 ORDER BY f.fact_kind, f.fact_id 

97 -- Zero means zero. Clamping an explicit 0 up to 1 would answer a question 

98 -- the caller did not ask, which §4.3 forbids: clamp, or reject, but never 

99 -- change what was asked into something else. 

100 LIMIT least( 

101 greatest(coalesce(facts_as_of.max_rows, {_MAX_ROWS_DEFAULT}), 0), 

102 {_MAX_ROWS_HARD_CAP} 

103 ) 

104$$; 

105"""
