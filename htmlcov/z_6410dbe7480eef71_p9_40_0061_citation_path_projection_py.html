1"""D140 §7: citation paths traverse the current reference projection. 

2 

3The graph's citation edge is one lineage-to-lineage edge per 

4``(from_doc_id, to_doc_id, kind)`` among the resolved references made by the 

5versions in force now (``rememberstack_graph_internal.crossrefs_live``). The 

6``memory_v1.graph_citation_path`` helper written by p9_17_0038 still expanded 

7``memory_v1.document_crossrefs_live`` row by row. Since D140 that view holds 

8the references of every non-tombstoned version, so a path could follow a 

9citation that only a superseded or repealed wording made, and two references 

10between the same documents gave two copies of one path (#520). 

11 

12Two changes: 

13 

14- ``crossrefs_live`` keeps its columns and its representative row (the 

15 smallest contributing ``crossref_id``, with that row's context and creation 

16 clock) and gains ``bindings`` and ``source_labels``: the distinct ``binding`` 

17 values and the distinct non-null ``source_label`` values of every reference 

18 the edge merges, sorted (labels in byte order). Without them an edge would 

19 show only its smallest row's qualifiers, and a pinned or caller-flagged 

20 citation merged into it would read as a plain one. The property graph's 

21 edge properties are unchanged, so the view is replaced in place. 

22- ``graph_citation_path`` expands that projection instead of the public 

23 per-reference view. Its signature, result columns, budgets, comment and 

24 grants are unchanged; it is replaced in place. 

25 

26The downgrade restores the p9_17 helper body and the p9_38 projection, which 

27drops the two columns; the property graph that depends on the view is dropped 

28and recreated around it with its grants. 

29"""

43GRAPH_CROSSREFS_PROJECTION_DDL = r""" 

44CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW rememberstack_graph_internal.crossrefs_live AS 

45SELECT x.deployment_id, 

46 (array_agg(x.crossref_id ORDER BY x.crossref_id))[1] AS crossref_id, 

47 x.from_doc_id, x.to_doc_id, 

48 x.kind::text AS kind, 

49 (array_agg(x.context ORDER BY x.crossref_id))[1] AS context, 

50 (array_agg(x.created_at ORDER BY x.crossref_id))[1] AS created_at, 

51 array_agg(DISTINCT x.binding::text ORDER BY x.binding::text) AS bindings, 

52 coalesce( 

53 array_agg(DISTINCT x.source_label COLLATE "C" 

54 ORDER BY x.source_label COLLATE "C") 

55 FILTER (WHERE x.source_label IS NOT NULL), 

56 ARRAY[]::text[] 

57 ) AS source_labels 

58FROM document_crossrefs AS x 

59JOIN document_reference_generations AS generation 

60 ON generation.deployment_id = x.deployment_id 

61 AND generation.version_id = x.from_version_id 

62 AND generation.generation_id = x.generation_id 

63 AND generation.status = 'active' 

64JOIN document_version_scope AS scope 

65 ON scope.deployment_id = x.deployment_id 

66 AND scope.version_id = x.from_version_id 

67 AND scope.doc_id = x.from_doc_id 

68 AND scope.selectable 

69 AND scope.in_force @> statement_timestamp() 

70JOIN documents AS target 

71 ON target.deployment_id = x.deployment_id 

72 AND target.doc_id = x.to_doc_id 

73 AND target.deleted_at IS NULL 

74WHERE x.resolved AND x.to_doc_id IS NOT NULL 

75GROUP BY x.deployment_id, x.from_doc_id, x.to_doc_id, x.kind; 

76""" 

77"""One lineage-to-lineage edge per (from_doc_id, to_doc_id, kind) among the 

78resolved references of the versions in force now, exactly as p9_38 projected 

79it, plus the qualifiers of every reference the edge merges. A filter on the 

80grouping columns (deployment and source lineage, as the helper probes) is 

81applied below the aggregation, so expansion still reads one lineage."""
