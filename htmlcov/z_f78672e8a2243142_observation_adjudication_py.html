33"""The observation adjudicator generation (D12; replayed on rebuild, D7). 

3407b pins temperature=0.0 — generation parameters are part of provenance. 

3509a (D106) adds the temporal-compatibility rung: two dated events with 

36disjoint resolved windows never collapse or supersede (they may only 

37contradict or stay distinct), a dated event never collapses as evidence onto 

38an undated statement (nor the reverse), identical text is collapsed only when 

39temporally compatible, open-ended windows stay unbounded, and the verdict 

40prompt shows both statements' said-on dates and is-about windows. 

4109b (D107 §5, WP-T.0a) compares canonical half-open bounds: a day covers 

42the whole calendar day, an instant is a non-empty point, adjacent units do 

43not overlap."""

45_VERDICT_PROMPT: Final = """You adjudicate observations for a memory system. 

46Both statements are believed facts about the SAME entity: 

47 

48EXISTING: {existing!r} 

49 said on: {existing_said_on} 

50 is about: {existing_about} 

51NEW: {new!r} 

52 said on: {new_said_on} 

53 is about: {new_about} 

54 

55Two clocks are shown for each statement. "said on" is the source's own date — 

56when the document was written or the conversation took place — and is NOT 

57when the described thing happened. "is about" is the world-time the statement 

58refers to, resolved from the source's wording against its said-on date: "last 

59week" said on 2022-10-06 is about the week before that date, not the week 

60before 2022-01-21, so two statements can both say "last week" and be about 

61days months apart. When the source tied nothing to a date, "is about" says so. 

62When either statement was ingested is irrelevant here and is not shown. 

63 

64Judge semantically (there are no typed columns — "FY2023" vs "fiscal 2023" 

65and "headcount" vs "staff count" are your equivalence calls): 

66- evidence: same property, same value, overlapping validity — the new 

67 statement re-asserts the existing one. 

68- supersede: same property, a CHANGING EFFECTIVE STATE (headcount, balance, 

69 status), and the value changed over time — the old window should cap. 

70 NEVER supersede a fixed-period measurement ("FY2023 revenue was $5M"): a 

71 figure does not stop being true at period-end. 

72- contradict: same property AND same reporting period, incompatible value — 

73 both must stand, surfaced together. (Different property, or different 

74 period, is NOT a contradiction.) 

75- new: a different property, period, or thing — no interaction. 

76 

77Time is decisive for EVENTS (a win, a visit, a purchase, a meeting). Two 

78statements about datable events whose "is about" windows do NOT overlap are 

79two different occurrences — `new` — even when the wording is identical ("won 

80a tournament last week" said in January and again in October are two wins, 

81not one re-asserted). The one exception: when they plainly name the SAME 

82single occurrence and merely disagree about its date ("the Valorant final on 

83Friday" vs "the Valorant final on Saturday"), answer `contradict` so both 

84stand. Overlapping windows of different precision (a year-level claim and a 

85day-level one) may well be the same occurrence — judge by the wording. A 

86specific dated event is never `evidence` for a vaguer summary ("has won a 

87few tournaments"), and a summary never re-asserts a specific event — keep 

88both."""

226@dataclass(frozen=True) 

227class _ClaimTiming: 

228 """What the D41 record says about WHEN one piece of testimony applies. 

229 

230 Windows are D107 §5 canonical half-open intervals: ``about_from`` is the 

231 inclusive start aligned to the claim's precision unit, ``about_until`` the 

232 EXCLUSIVE end (``None`` = open). Two clocks. ``asserted_at`` is when the SOURCE said it — the document's 

233 or conversation's own timestamp (the supersession boundary the layer 

234 already used). ``about_from``/``about_until`` is the world-time the 

235 statement is ABOUT, resolved by the extractor from the source's wording 

236 against that date, and ``about_kind`` is the D41 ``claim_valid_kind`` that 

237 says what sort of interval it is: ``event_time`` (a datable event — the 

238 only kind the temporal-compatibility rung acts on), ``measurement_period`` 

239 / ``effective_period`` / ``proposition_validity`` (a figure or state tied 

240 to a span), or ``period`` for a block row whose supporting claims are 

241 aggregated. All three are ``None`` when the source tied nothing to a date. 

242 When the statement was ingested is deliberately not part of this record. 

243 """
