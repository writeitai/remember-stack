1"""Extract eligibility: which strings may become entity names (D95 WP-I.1). 

2 

3Bare head nouns are not referents. ``game`` is not FIFA 23. The check is 

4deterministic so ingest does not depend on the LLM obeying the prompt. 

5A ``source`` alias must also appear in the claim so a hallucinated surface 

6cannot poison T0. 

7 

8The helper is used from resolve, not only from E3. A document's own name, 

9written into a claim in place of a self-reference, is not a referent either 

10(D134): ``own_document_name_slot`` finds the one reference to skip.
