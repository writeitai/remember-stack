1"""The managed meter measures any cost class, in the convert stage. 

2 

3revision: p9_46_0067 

4 

5The measurement outbox becomes the generic v3 receipt: a ``cost_class``, an 

6integer ``native_quantity`` in that class's ``unit`` and the ``format_family``, 

7in place of a text-only character count. Measurement moves from ingest into 

8the convert stage, so: 

9 

10- uploads write their original to the raw store at ingest, and the outbox 

11 no longer stages bytes (``staged_content`` is dropped); 

12- unchanged bytes create no version and are never measured, so ``no_op`` 

13 measurements and outcomes are deleted and the disposition column dropped; 

14- a version has at most one measurement; 

15- convert work waiting for the control plane's holds parks with the new 

16 ``admission`` defer reason, which no claim takes until approval releases it. 

17 

18Refuses to run while any measurement still holds staged bytes: those 

19originals exist nowhere else. Drain the outbox with the previous release 

20first. The downgrade restores the text-only shape while the outbox holds 

21only delivered text receipts and no work is parked for admission. 

22"""
